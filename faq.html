Frequently asked questions.

A few useful details about our website packages, process, and getting your project started.

What does RetailX build?

We build fast, mobile-first static websites and landing pages for businesses, startups, retail brands, and service providers. The design focuses on explaining your offer clearly and helping visitors contact you.

How much does a website cost?

The website package starts at ₹1599. Custom domain registration is billed separately.

How long does a website take to launch?

Our stated workflow is five days: briefing on day one, building on days two and three, review on day four, and launch on day five. Timing depends on receiving the information and feedback needed for your project.

Is a domain included in the price?

A custom domain is billed separately. A free RetailX subdomain option is also available.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Share your business details, goals, the sections you need, and any available logo, images, or references. No assets yet? You can leave the asset link blank and we’ll follow up.

How do I start a project?

Fill out the project brief on our homepage. It opens WhatsApp with your brief pre-filled so you can review and send it to us.

Can I review the website before it launches?

Yes. The process includes a review and refinement stage before launch so you can share feedback on the site.
